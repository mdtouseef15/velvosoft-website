About us:

Who we are:

Velvosoft is a Bangalore-based hygiene product manufacturer focused on creating tissue products and hygiene cleaning solutions that support cleaner, healthier everyday spaces. Our approach is rooted in quality, consistency, and responsible manufacturing.

From comfort-focused tissue products to effective cleaning solutions, we design our range to deliver reliable performance for homes, businesses, and commercial environments. At Velvosoft, we believe hygiene is not just a product — it’s a responsibility we take seriously.
